Privacy policy

Personal Calendar Sync · Updated November 3, 2026

Access and purpose

This personal macOS utility uses Google OAuth to read the authorized user's calendar list and events and to create, update, and delete busy-time copies in selected destination calendars. Calendar information is used solely to perform and verify synchronization requested by the owner.

Data handling

Event times, response status, identifiers, and descriptions are processed to select events, avoid synchronization loops, and maintain copies. Destination copies use generic titles such as Personal commitment or Occupied rather than original event details. Local activity history may include original event titles and calendar names so the owner can inspect synchronization.

OAuth credentials, synchronization state, settings, and activity history are stored on the owner's Mac. Credential files are restricted to the owner's operating-system user. Calendar data and credentials are not published on this website or in the source repository.

Sharing and retention

The utility communicates with Google APIs to read and write the calendars selected by the owner. Busy-time copies are visible according to those destination calendars' existing sharing settings. It does not send calendar data to this website, analytics providers, advertisers, or AI model services, and does not sell calendar data or use it for advertising or model training.

Local activity history is limited to the latest 30 completed runs. Local settings, credentials, and synchronization state remain until removed by the owner. Google event copies remain until removed by synchronization or deleted by the owner.

Control

The owner can pause synchronization in the local control panel and revoke Google authorization through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking authorization prevents future API access but does not automatically remove existing event copies or local files.

Personal Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
