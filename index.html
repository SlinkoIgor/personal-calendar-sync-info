Personal Calendar Sync

A private macOS utility that keeps busy-time blocks consistent between calendars selected by its owner.

The utility reads selected Google Calendar events and creates, updates, and removes its own generic busy-time copies. It runs locally on its owner's Mac, verifies changes directly with Google, and retries failed operations.

This is a personal application, not a public calendar service. These public pages contain information about the utility only. They do not provide access to calendars or synchronization controls.
